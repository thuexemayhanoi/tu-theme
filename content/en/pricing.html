The cost of renting a motorbike in Hanoi with Mr Tu depends on two things: the model you choose and how long you keep it, whether that's a day, a week or a month. The deposit is a separate amount, refunded under your rental agreement, and Mr Tu confirms both the price and the deposit before you book.

Prices are in Vietnamese dong (VND). If a price is marked as a reference price, or appears as "on request", Mr Tu confirms the exact amount when you contact us. Nothing is final until it has been agreed and written into your rental agreement.

How daily, weekly and monthly rates work

Every model has a daily, a weekly and a monthly rate. Use the tabs above the table to compare the same bike across all three periods.

  • Daily: for a day out or a short stay. See daily and weekly rental.
  • Weekly: for seven days in a row. If you need a bike for five or six days, compare it with the daily rate before you decide.
  • Monthly: for longer stays, work or study. See monthly motorbike rental.

Why isn't a month 30 times the daily price?

Because weekly and monthly rates are prices in their own right, not multiples of the daily rate. Renting a bike out for a month means one hand-over, one inspection and one agreement instead of many, so longer periods are priced differently. The per-day cost therefore tends to fall as the period gets longer, which is why it pays to compare the tabs before you book several single days in a row.

How the deposit works

The deposit is not part of the rental price. It is a separate sum that secures the bike while it is in your hands, and you get it back when you return the bike as set out in your rental agreement.

  • The amount depends on the model. You'll find it next to each bike in the table, and Mr Tu confirms it before you book.
  • The form is agreed in advance. How you leave the deposit, for example in cash, is agreed with you before booking.
  • Identity documents are checked for the rental agreement. That check is separate from the deposit, in line with UK Foreign Office advice not to hand over a passport as a deposit when hiring a vehicle.
  • The refund follows the agreement. When you bring the bike back, you check it together. If there is damage, the cost is assessed on the actual damage, as your agreement sets out.

The general rules every renter agrees to are on our rental terms page.

What else might you pay for?

The rental price covers the bike for the agreed period. A few running costs are yours, and the agreement makes clear which ones:

  • Fuel. You pay for the fuel you use. The fuel level is noted at hand-over and again when you return the bike.
  • Parking. Markets, sights, cafés and many buildings have paid parking (gửi xe). Keep the ticket, because you'll need it to collect the bike.
  • Traffic fines. Any fine incurred during your rental is your responsibility, as confirmed in the agreement.
  • Delivery, if arranged. If you'd like the bike brought to where you're staying, ask first. Mr Tu confirms whether that's possible and what it costs, and any delivery cost is written into the agreement.

Rent, deposit and any agreed extra are all in writing before you ride off.

How to keep your costs down

  • Match the period to your stay. If you need a bike for most of a week or a month, compare that rate with the same number of single days.
  • Check the bike carefully at hand-over. Photos of existing marks keep the record clear, so nobody is in any doubt about damage that was already there.
  • Park only where it's allowed. Fines for parking in the wrong place are yours to pay, so use attended parking when in doubt.
  • Return on time, or call ahead. Late returns are handled as your agreement sets out, so let Mr Tu know early if your plans change.

Prices and deposit: common questions

How much does it cost to rent a motorbike in Hanoi?

It depends on the model and the rental period. The table above lists Mr Tu's daily, weekly and monthly rates and the deposit for each bike, and Mr Tu confirms the exact price for your dates before you book.

Is the deposit included in the rental price?

No. The deposit is a separate amount that depends on the model, and it is refunded when you return the bike, under the terms of your rental agreement.

Is a weekly rental cheaper than paying day by day?

Usually, when you work it out per day. The weekly rate is set on its own rather than calculated from the daily one, so check both tabs for your model before you decide.

Are there any extra fees?

Only what is written in your agreement. Fuel, parking and any traffic fines during your rental are yours to pay, and if delivery is arranged, its cost is agreed and written down in advance.
