This policy explains what personal data the Mr Tu website handles, why, and what choices you have. In short: the site sets no advertising cookies, the help assistant runs on this website without any third-party AI, and when you call or message us, we use your details only to answer you and arrange your rental.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Who is responsible

This website is run by Mr Tu, a motorbike rental service near Hanoi's Old Quarter. Mr Tu is responsible for the personal data described in this policy and decides how it is used. You can reach us using the details on our contact page.

What we process and why

Visits to the website

Like any website, ours is served by a hosting provider whose servers keep technical logs of each request: typically your IP address, the date and time, the page requested, and your browser and device type. These logs are used to deliver the site, keep it secure and fix errors. We do not use them to build profiles of visitors. How long they are kept depends on the hosting provider's retention rules.

Calls and messages from you

When you call or message Mr Tu, we receive your phone number and whatever you choose to tell us, such as your name, the bike you want, your dates and where you are staying. We use this only to answer your questions and to arrange and carry out your rental. We keep it only as long as we need it for that, or as long as the law requires.

Your rental agreement

If you rent a bike, your identity document and licence are checked, and the details needed for the written rental agreement are recorded in it. They are used to carry out the agreement and to meet our legal obligations.

The help assistant on this site

The help assistant answers common questions about renting. Your questions are processed on this website itself and are not sent to any third-party AI service. If the assistant cannot answer a question, the text of that question may be stored anonymously for up to 30 days so that we can improve the answers. It is stored without your IP address or contact details.

Please don't type personal information, such as your passport number or phone number, into the assistant. If you would like us to help with your booking, call us instead.

Your colour-mode preference

If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage (localStorage) so that the site remembers it next time. This is not a cookie and it is never sent to us. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Cookies

The theme and plugins that run this website set no advertising cookies. WordPress, the software behind the site, may set technical cookies for administrators who log in to manage it. You don't need to log in to use the site, so as a visitor you won't receive them.

Our commitment: this policy describes the website as it runs today. Before we add analytics or any other third-party tool, we will update this policy to explain what it does with your data.

Links to other websites

Some pages link to other websites, such as Google Maps or official travel advice. Once you follow a link, that site's own privacy policy applies, not this one. Tapping a phone link opens your phone's dialler; the call itself is handled by your phone provider.

Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only:

  • with our hosting provider, to run the website;
  • with a competent authority, where the law requires it;
  • with anyone else only when you ask us to.

Your rights

Under applicable Vietnamese law on personal data protection, including the Law on Personal Data Protection in force since 1 January 2026, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • give you a copy of that data;
  • correct it if it is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete it, or limit how we use it, where the law allows;
  • stop using it if you withdraw your consent.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to confirm who you are before acting on it, and we will reply within the time the law allows. You can also complain to the competent Vietnamese authority.

Keeping data safe

We keep only the personal data we need, limit who can see it and take reasonable steps to protect it. No website or phone service is completely secure, so please don't send copies of documents unless we ask for them.

Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the website or the law changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
